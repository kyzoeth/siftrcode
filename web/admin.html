⚖️ Empirical Promotion Policy & Scientific Integrity

SiftrCode requires learned ranking models to demonstrate statistically significant, point-in-time superiority over the frozen deterministic baseline (ΔNDCG@10 > +0.02, win/loss > 1.0) on frozen natural holdouts before promotion. Because candidate gbdt_pairwise_v1 recorded ΔNDCG@10 = -0.0166 with 3 wins vs 6 losses, it remains strictly labeled Research. The production engine remains 100% deterministic ContextRank.
